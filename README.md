> **"Redefining gravity-based storage through the buoyancy of airships."**

This project introduces an innovative **Gravity Energy Storage System (GESS)** that utilizes the buoyant force of Helium-filled airships to store and release renewable energy. Developed at the **Samrat Ashok Technological Institute (SATI), Vidisha**, this research provides a mechanical alternative to chemical batteries, aiming for higher sustainability and long-term efficiency [cite: 1, 182, 184].

---

## 🚀 The Concept: How it Works

The system operates as a mechanical battery, utilizing **Buoyant Force ($F_b$)** instead of heavy weights [cite: 16].

### 🔋 Energy Storage (Charging)
When there is excess energy on the grid (e.g., from solar or wind power), the system uses this energy to power electric motors that pull down the airship [cite: 322]. The energy is stored as potential energy against the air's buoyant force [cite: 323, 334].

### 🔌 Energy Release (Discharging)
When energy is needed, the airship is released [cite: 325]. It is connected to generators that convert the stored potential energy into electrical energy as the airship rises to its maximum height [cite: 325].

## 📊 Technical Specifications & Calculations

Based on the research for a medium-sized airship, the following parameters were established:

| Parameter | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Airship Volume ($V$)** | $15,000 \, \text{m}^3$ [cite: 43, 112] |
| **Air Density ($\rho_{\text{air}}$)** | $1.225 \, \text{kg/m}^3$ [cite: 34, 111] |
| **Helium Density ($\rho_{\text{he}}$)** | $0.1786 \, \text{kg/m}^3$ [cite: 108] |
| **Total Buoyant Force ($F_T$)** | $153,900 \, \text{N}$ [cite: 73, 117] |
| **Maximum Altitude** | $3,000 \, \text{m}$ [cite: 90, 118] |
| **Potential Energy Stored** | $461,700,000 \, \text{J}$ [cite: 123] |
| **Electrical Output (at 85% Efficiency)** | $\approx 109 \, \text{kWh}$ [cite: 125, 131] |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Physics:** The buoyant force is generated by the difference between the density of air and the gas inside the airship [cite: 16]. The formula used is $F_{b}=(\rho_{\text{air}}-\rho_{\text{helium}}) \cdot g \cdot V$ [cite: 114].

## 🌟 Key Advantages

* **♻️ Sustainable:** Avoids the environmental impact and safety risks of lithium-ion batteries, such as thermal runaway [cite: 232, 239].
* **⏳ Long Lifespan:** Mechanical systems offer decades of operation without the degradation seen in chemical batteries [cite: 331, 332].
* **📉 Efficient Storage:** Can store energy over long durations without significant losses [cite: 337].
* **🌍 Geographic Freedom:** Unlike pumped hydro, it does not require specific topography like mountains or large water reservoirs [cite: 251, 252].

## 🛠️ Environmental Analysis

The research analyzes how environmental factors affect the airship [cite: 132]:
* **Temperature:** As altitude increases, temperature decreases, causing the internal gas to contract and increase in density [cite: 140, 141].
* **Pressure:** Decreasing pressure at altitude causes gas expansion, which helps generate sufficient buoyant force [cite: 157, 158, 160].
* **Wind:** While wind affects the ship, engineering experience makes it a manageable factor [cite: 163, 165].
